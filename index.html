Trial visualization

The viewer applies light temporal smoothing to the displayed MANO vertices and interpolates between adjacent meshes at video timestamps. Completion flags identify filled mesh frames. The pressure map and surface force share the pressure2 timeline and retain the original force values and face assignments. The pressure map uses 3×5-cell averages, and regional force is smoothed separately.

Capture and Annotations

Each hand was recorded in two independent wristband donnings. For each donning, the protocol called for five repetitions of each of 34 contact configurations.

MyoBridge

MyoBridge fuses visual and sEMG features by hand region and distributes regional force over predicted contact vertices.
